Common questions

Questions we get asked a lot

Straight answers to what most owners want to know before they call.

How much does custom software cost for a small business?

Our Prototype Sprint is a fixed $5,000 to $15,000, and a full build is usually quoted in the tens of thousands depending on scope. We price the sprint first so you get a real number for the full build before committing to it, instead of an open-ended hourly arrangement.

How long does it take to build custom software?

A Prototype Sprint takes one to three weeks and produces something you can actually use. A full build typically runs a few months, depending on how much it needs to do and how many systems it has to connect to. The sprint exists so you find out which it is before you commit.

Should I build custom software or just use an off-the-shelf product?

Use off-the-shelf software when it fits, because it is cheaper and faster. Custom makes sense when the tools you already pay for do not talk to each other, when you are paying staff to copy data between systems, or when the way you work is genuinely different from how the software assumes you work. If an existing product would solve your problem, we will tell you so.

Do I own the code you write?

Yes. Everything we build is yours outright, including anything produced during the Prototype Sprint. If you stop after the sprint, you keep it, and you are free to take it to another developer.

What happens if I stop after the prototype?

Nothing happens. There is no penalty and no obligation to continue, and you keep everything we built. If you do continue, the sprint fee comes off the price of the full build.

Can you integrate with ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro?

Yes, and that is most of what we do for home services companies. We are not trying to replace the platform you already pay for. We build the pieces around it, like the connection to your accounting system or the reporting it does not offer out of the box.

Can you work alongside our practice management system?

Yes. For dental and medical practices we generally build around the existing system rather than replacing it, covering things like patient communication, custom reporting, and coordination between multiple locations. We handle patient data with the care that requires and will work within your compliance obligations.

Do you work with businesses outside Phoenix?

We focus on the Phoenix metro area and Arizona, because being local is a real part of what we offer: same time zone, meetings in person when they help, and one accountable person. We will consider work elsewhere, but being nearby is part of the point.

Who actually does the work?

Nathan Bailey, who founded Arizona Tech Labs after fifteen years building systems at Meta, Microsoft, Visa, and GoDaddy. You deal with him directly, and the person who builds your software is the person who maintains it. Nothing is sent offshore.
